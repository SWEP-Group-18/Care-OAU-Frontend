Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about using the Care-OAU student healthcare portal.

What is Care-OAU?

Care-OAU is a digital appointment and queue management web platform developed specifically for students of the Obafemi Awolowo University Health Centre to streamline clinic visits and clinical access.

Who is the platform intended for?

The platform is designed for registered undergraduate and postgraduate students of Obafemi Awolowo University who access healthcare services at the campus health centre.

How do I create an account?

Click "Create Account" on the header or hero section, fill in your full name, student matriculation number, phone number, valid email address, and choose a secure password.

Why do I need to verify my email?

Email verification ensures that only genuine students access campus healthcare services and protects your medical visit history with two-step validation.

How can I access available health-centre services?

Sign in to your student dashboard to book an appointment, check doctor duty schedules, or review current clinic operations and health tips.

What happens if doctor availability is not displayed?

Care-OAU strictly presents admin-published doctor rosters. If no records have been published yet or the clinical roster is updating, a clear notice is shown rather than fictitious fallback data.

What should I do if I cannot sign in?

Use the "Forgot password?" link on the sign-in page to request a secure password reset code to your registered email address.

How do I get help if a service is unavailable?

If you experience any difficulties or require emergency medical attention, visit the OAU Health Centre directly on campus during operational hours.
