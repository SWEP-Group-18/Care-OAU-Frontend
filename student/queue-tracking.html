Queue Protocol & Guidelines

1. Ticket Generation

Queue tickets are generated exclusively at the Health Centre reception following on-site check-in. Booking online reserves your slot but does not assign a ticket.

2. Audio & Visual Calling

Watch the reception callboard monitors and listen for your number when called. Please proceed directly to your assigned consulting room.

3. Missing Your Call

Patients who do not respond after three calls are flagged as "No Show" to prevent clinical bottlenecks. Report to reception triage if delayed.
